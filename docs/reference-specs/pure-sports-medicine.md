# Pure Sports Medicine: reference spec

> Source: https://puresportsmed.com/ · Crawled 2026-10-27 at 390, 768, 1280 and 1920 px · Category: Health & Yoga · Pages crawled: 8

## 1. Overview
Pure Sports Medicine is a London multidisciplinary sports medicine and physiotherapy group with ten clinics, aimed at everyone from elite athletes to casual exercisers. The site (WordPress, built by an agency) is calm, modern and soft: a warm cream page (#f8f7f2), deep petrol-teal panels (#002a30) with large rounded corners that rise over the band above, and pastel mint (#c6e4e1) and sky (#bee8ff) cards. Three things make it recognisable: every heading is set in a very light grotesk (TWK Lausanne 300) and split into two tones, part muted and part solid; every button, tab, tag and input is a full pill; and the hero carries a large pill-shaped booking bar (service, clinic, specialist, Book) laid over a looping video.

## 7. Motion and interaction
- Video: the Home and About heroes and several inner heroes use a muted autoplaying looping video.
- Carousels: 44 slider elements detected (services slider, patient stories, posts, management team, review widget), each with outlined circle arrows and a thin progress line.
- Marquees: the 100px service-name line and the recruitment line scroll sideways continuously; the insurer logo row in the stats panel also scrolls.
- Scroll effects: 22 elements are hidden at load and revealed on scroll; paragraphs in the philosophy block change word by word from muted to dark as they scroll into view.
- Hover: 251 elements carry CSS transitions; 3 CSS animations run. Exact hover styles were not measured.
- Accordions with "+" pills that turn into a filled "minus" pill when open.
- Sticky elements: none (the header scrolls away). No GSAP, Lenis, Locomotive, Lottie or Framer detected.
- Ignore the live-chat "?" bubble and the marketing popup overlay; they are not part of the design.

## 10. Imagery and icons
- Photography: real clinic and patient photography in bright, natural light: clinicians working hands-on with patients, gym and rehab floors, runners outdoors, clinic interiors and building exteriors. Warm, friendly, diverse ages. No moody filters.
- Treatment: radius about 16px on photos, 24px on collage photos with soft shadows, never sharp corners. Image cards use a dark bottom fade for white labels.
- Video: hero backgrounds (pool, gym) muted and desaturated toward blue-grey; an overview video with a round white play button on About.
- Icons: thin line icons in teal inside outlined circles (feature rows), small line icons on stat cards and booking selects, outlined arrow chips, white social glyphs.
- Logos: insurer logos in white on dark; review platform logo in colour.
- Not used: illustrations, 3D renders, gradients as backgrounds (only image fades), emoji, heavy drop shadows on panels, sharp-cornered boxes.

## 13. Dos and don'ts
- **Do:** set every heading in a light grotesk (weight 300) with negative tracking, and split it into a solid part and a 50% part of the same colour.
- **Do:** make every interactive element a pill: buttons, tabs, tags, inputs, the booking bar, accordion toggles.
- **Do:** alternate the cream page with full-width #002a30 panels that have 32 to 40px corners and overlap the section above.
- **Do:** use mint #c6e4e1 and sky #bee8ff as the only light accent surfaces, often alternating within one row of cards.
- **Do:** round all photos (16px) and use bright, real clinical and outdoor photography.
- **Do:** give carousels outlined circular arrows and a thin progress line rather than dots.
- **Do:** put a booking bar in the hero rather than a single button.
- **Don't:** use bold headings or uppercase headings; uppercase is only for 9px labels.
- **Don't:** use sharp corners or square buttons anywhere.
- **Don't:** introduce a saturated brand colour for buttons; actions are dark teal, cream or outlined.
- **Don't:** put heavy shadows on panels; only the small collage photos carry a soft shadow.
- **Don't:** make the header sticky or solid on the home page at desktop width; it floats transparent over the video.
